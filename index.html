    Clear Skies

    About

    See where the planets will be over your skyline.

    Paths are dashed below the horizon and in daylight.

    Built with Astronomy Engine. Overlays are typically accurate to within 1° for official Street View imagery (user-submitted imagery may be highly inaccurate).

    Settings

    Sets when each object counts as observable.

    Uranus and Neptune stay off unless you switch them on.

    Stars and constellations after sunset, dimmed behind buildings and trees. Reloads the page.

    Hides stars too faint for the place's light pollution.
